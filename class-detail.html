Italian Handcrafted Pasta & Emulsion Lab

Master 00 flour hydration, handmade tagliatelle, agnolotti dal plin, and silky emulsified butter-sage reduction.

Duration: 3.5 Hours
Instructor: Chef Marco Rossi
Level: Starter
Overview

Class Introduction

Making extraordinary pasta is not about mechanical repetition; it is a sensory dialogue with humidity, flour protein content, egg yolk viscosity, and kinetic movement. In this 3.5-hour workshop, Chef Marco Rossi strips away culinary mystery to teach you the authentic craft of northern Italian pasta makers.

Each student operates at their own dedicated maple wood cutting island with commercial bronze-die hand rollers, custom brass pasta cutters, and induction pans for sauce emulsion.

The Menu

What You'll Cook

Silky Tagliatelle al Tartufo

Hand-rolled egg yolk ribbon pasta with summer black truffle reduction and 24-month Parmigiano.

Piedmontese Agnolotti dal Plin

Pinched pocket pasta filled with roasted butternut squash, nutmeg, and cultured butter-sage glaze.

Technique Matrix

Techniques You'll Learn

01
Gluten Web Hydration: Calculating 57% hydration ratios across seasonal ambient humidity fluctuations.
02
Zero-Stretch Sheeting: Wood dowel rolling and manual reduction without tearing delicate sheets.
03
Mantecatura Emulsion: Starch-binding emulsion physics creating high-gloss sauces without heavy cream.
Provenance

Ingredients Matter

We source stone-ground organic Caputo 00 soft wheat flour imported directly from Naples, pastured organic golden-yolk eggs from local family farms, 24-month Vacche Rosse Parmigiano-Reggiano, and hand-foraged seasonal black truffles.
